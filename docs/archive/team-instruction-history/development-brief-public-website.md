# Public Website Production Sprint — Development Brief

## Objective
Upgrade the public Echo11 website into a production-level business website that feels credible, clear, and commercially strong. This sprint focuses only on what public visitors see on the marketing website. It does **not** include /lab, /portal, or /client surfaces.

## Current business reality
Echo11 is still early in its growth stage, so the public website must reflect the work that is live, credible, and currently representative.

### Active case studies to keep
- **TrueLuxe Nepal**
- **The Leaders NP**
- **curriculum.os** — https://curriculum-os-tan.vercel.app

### Case studies to disable for now
These should be removed from public-facing listings, homepage selected work, and any marketing references until they are active and business-approved again.
- Prika Couture
- Green Lifestyle
- StudentStack
- 3am3d

## Product standards for this sprint
The public site must:
- sound natural, calm, and credible
- avoid dramatic, artificial, or over-generated wording
- make Echo11 easy to understand within seconds
- build trust with clear positioning and proof
- guide visitors toward meaningful conversion actions
- be strong for SEO and AI discoverability
- feel like a launched business, not an experiment

## Core goals
1. Clarify what Echo11 does and who it serves
2. Improve trust and business legitimacy
3. Strengthen conversion paths and inquiry quality
4. Improve SEO structure and AI retrievability
5. Refine public case study presentation

## Public website pages in scope
- /

- /work/[slug]

- /methodology
- /manifesto
- /contact
- shared public layout pieces like navbar, footer, CTA, selected work, services grid, SEO metadata

## Shared direction for all contributors
- Read your task fully before starting.
- Understand the business context first, then propose solutions.
- Use real-world references from strong digital product studios, agencies, and B2B service companies.
- Prioritize clarity over stylistic cleverness.
- Be creative, but keep recommendations realistic and production-ready.
- Do not introduce fake proof, fake clients, fake metrics, or placeholder links.
- When your task is complete, update your task document with your findings, recommendations, and final output so it can serve as a shared meeting artifact / moodboard.

## Shared business message to protect
Echo11 should come across as a product engineering studio that helps businesses plan, design, and build websites, platforms, and digital products with clear process and dependable execution.

## Deliverable expectation
Every team artifact should help the final website become:
- clearer
- more trustworthy
- more commercially effective
- easier to find through search and AI-driven discovery
