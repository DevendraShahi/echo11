# Echo11 showcase — 20-reference study

Reviewed 10 October 2026. This is a curated reference set, not a verified global ranking. Official studio sites were opened for their positioning, work structure, navigation, and available visual presentation. Some immersive sites exposed a loading screen or very little accessible content; their full interactive choreography and video reels were not verified. The recommendations below are Echo11 design interpretations, not claims that every reference implements the same interaction.

## Chosen Echo11 direction

**One studio. Three distinct voices.** The viewer follows the move from an intention to a designed website. FORGE expresses force; LALTIN gives place and stillness room; pizzaslice expresses appetite and approachable character.

Each chapter has two frames:

1. **The idea:** an editorial identity composition, a source image, and a concise explanation of the brand intention.
2. **The website:** an uncropped page capture, three selectable design explanations (Voice, Atmosphere, Journey), and the live experience.

The image wipe reveals the execution; it is a transition between story beats, not an ambient floating effect. Chapter timing varies modestly: FORGE settles faster, LALTIN slower, pizzaslice between them. No continuous float, pointer tilt, autoplay audio, simulated awards, or invented client results.

Persistent chapter navigation shows two progress marks per concept. An optional finite Play sequence advances through the remaining frames and ends on the final website. Visitors can stop it, change chapters, choose an explanation, or open the preview. Keyboard, wheel, and touch advance the sequence on roomy viewports. Short/narrow viewports and reduced-motion preferences use a normal document with instant frame changes.

Live previews retain Desktop/Mobile widths, Escape to close, focus return, and a full-site link. The concepts remain labelled as studio-owned explorations; the restaurant menu, locations, and ordering remain illustrative.
