# Echo11: a signal becoming clear

Applied to the shared inner-page atmosphere and the main marketing page openings. The half-circle is a responsive cyan horizon, with one finite arrival and a reading fade. Reduced motion renders it statically. The old six-second infinite gradient pulse is removed.

Services, About, Process, Make it yours, Contact and Work share an opening spacing rule that leaves a quiet area before the headline. Existing fonts, content, navigation, forms and user-approved compositions remain. About and Process passive stage numbers are neutral; functional cyan states remain.

Services supporting hero geometry is smaller and quieter. Its connected customer journey appears in order, once, below the introduction. The chosen Services article layouts and the rebuilt closing invitation remain. The homepage keeps its existing wordmark signal field; no extra semicircle is added to it.

The intended experience is curiosity, understanding, confidence, then a clear action. This is a visual principle, not a new claim about business results.

Placement correction: the horizon now has its own sky band. A restrained ground line sits below it, and opening content starts 24px beneath the line. Responsive sky-height and gap variables keep this relationship together. Desktop and mobile use different sky proportions. Motion visibly widens and lifts the light over 2.4 seconds, then settles; the ground draws from the centre. Additional inner-page openings now use the same spacing.

Final horizon choice: restored the original circular gradient, soft blur, and 35% opening opacity. Diameter scales from 512px on mobile to the original 1000px on desktop; reserved sky space follows that diameter, with a separate gap before content. Removed the horizontal rule and ellipse mask. Motion uses uniform scale so the circle stays circular.
